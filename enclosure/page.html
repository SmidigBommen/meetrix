Design decisions

  • The top is the control. Two keys cover it edge to edge. A thin seam and a shadow line are the only details.
  • Press anywhere. Four switches and four hooks per key make every point click, corners included.
  • All black. The case steps back and the light is what you see.
  • Color marks function. Orange is used once, on the plus. The minus is off-white.
  • Clean front. No logo, no text, no screws. Smoked acrylic hides the LED grid when off.
  • Long life. No glue. The panel is clamped and the keys snap in, so each part can be replaced.

Check before printing

  • Panel size. The model assumes 128 × 128 × 15 mm (4 mm pitch). Measure yours and change panel and panelDepth.
  • Board position. The controller is drawn as a rough block. Check that it clears the four pillars and reaches the cable exit.
  • Switches. Sized for 6 × 6 × 5 mm tactile switches with 0.3 mm travel. Other heights need a new switchH.
  • Snap hooks. Print the keys in PETG. The hooks bend 0.6 mm on assembly and PLA may crack.
  • Wall mounting. The back sits flush on the wall, which covers the vent holes. Use 5 mm spacers behind the screws.
  • Not printed yet. Parts are checked for collisions in software only. Expect one test print for tolerances.
